Detailed Description

The wheel joint can be used to simulate wheels on vehicles.

The wheel joint restricts body B to move along a local axis in body A. Body B is free to rotate. Supports a linear spring, linear limits, and a rotational motor.

Data Structure Documentation

◆ b2WheelJointDef

struct b2WheelJointDef

Wheel joint definition Body B is a wheel that may rotate freely and slide along the local x-axis in frame A.

The joint translation is zero when the local frame origins coincide in world space.
