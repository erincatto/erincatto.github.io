◆ b2MoverJointDef

struct b2MoverJointDef

A mover joint is used to move a dynamic character mover through velocity commands.

The x and y directions are handled separately. Does not affect rotation.

Data Fields
b2JointDef base Base joint definition.
int internalValue Used internally to detect a valid definition. DO NOT SET.
b2Vec2 linearVelocity The desired linear velocity.
b2Vec2 maxVelocityForce The maximum motor force in newtons.
