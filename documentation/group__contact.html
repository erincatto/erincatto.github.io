struct b2ContactData

The contact data for two shapes.

By convention the manifold normal points from shape A to shape B.

See also
b2Shape_GetContactData() and b2Body_GetContactData()
Data Fields
b2ContactId contactId The contact id.

You may hold onto this to track a contact across time steps. This id may become orphaned. Use b2Contact_IsValid before using it for other functions.

b2Manifold manifold The manifold copied from the contact.
b2ShapeId shapeIdA The first shape id.
b2ShapeId shapeIdB The second shape id.
